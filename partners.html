The Power of Partnership

The Confluence Camp Retreat is convened by The Brooks Ministry as a non-profit endeavor. We serve university students and young adults across the nation with standard accommodations, balanced daily feeding, and study resources at zero cost to them.

This mandate is fully funded by individuals and corporate bodies who share our vision to see a generation raised in fire, character, and alignment. Partner with us to keep the fire burning on the altar.
