✦ RETREAT EXPERIENCE

The Camp Experience

What awaits you at The Confluence Camp 2026? A sacred space structured to help you disconnect from the world and connect with Heaven.

We gather not for entertainment, but for an encounter. Through structured prayer watches, study sessions, and fellowship, we host the presence of God. Prepare your heart.

3 Days Consecrated Devotion
8 Watches Of Prayer & Worship
500+ Campers Confluenced

Spiritual Pillars

The Core Parameters

Worship Sessions

Corporate devotion led by anointed worship ministers. These sessions are structured to create a path into the throne room, elevating your spirit to behold His glory.

Teaching & Word

Facilitators will share teachings focused on spiritual growth, sound doctrines, Christian leadership, and purpose in the marketplace.

Intercessory Prayers

Intensive hours of prayer, spiritual building, and personal intercession. We pray to build spiritual capacity and break demonic yokes.

Welfare & Lodging

Accommodation & Feeding

We believe that spiritual focus requires freedom from logistical worries. To support your retreat experience, The Brooks Ministry provides accommodation and feeding for all registered attendees throughout the retreat, completely free of charge.

Separate hostels are secured for male and female campers with standard facilities within the Redemption City of God. Our welfare team coordinates three daily balanced meals to ensure every camper remains physically energized.
